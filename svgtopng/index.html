How to Convert SVG to PNG

  1. Upload SVG files, or switch to "Paste Code" and paste the SVG source directly.
  2. Choose the export size, background color and scale.
  3. Click “Convert PNG” to generate previews, then download individual PNG files or a ZIP.

FAQ

Are files uploaded when converting SVG to PNG?

No. SVG parsing, Canvas rendering and ZIP packaging all happen locally in your browser.

Why does the transparent background look like a checkerboard?

The checkerboard is only a preview aid for transparent areas. The downloaded PNG keeps its alpha channel.

How can I export a sharper PNG?

Choose a larger export size, or use 2x / 3x scale to generate a higher-resolution PNG.

Why are images, fonts, or some content missing?

For security reasons, browsers do not load external images, fonts, or stylesheets when drawing an SVG, and HTML inside foreignObject may not render. Embed images as data URLs and convert text to paths before converting.
