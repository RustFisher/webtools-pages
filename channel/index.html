Phase TestStereo

In phase should sound stable and centered; out of phase sounds thinner and wider, useful for checking wiring or channel processing.

TroubleshootingHeadphones
  • Only the left ear should play the left channel, and only the right ear should play the right channel.
  • If left and right are swapped, check headphone orientation or system channel settings.
  • If one side is silent, check the plug, Bluetooth connection or channel balance.
